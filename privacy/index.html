SimNewTab Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

SimNewTab ("this extension") is a browser new tab page extension. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how the extension handles data.

Overview

This extension has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking code.

All of your personalization is stored only in your own browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local) and is never uploaded to our servers. While running, the extension makes requests to the public third-party services listed below only to provide the corresponding features, and never sends any information that could identify you personally.

Data Stored Locally

The following data stays on your device (unless you explicitly use the export feature):

  • Shortcut list (names, URLs, icons and colors)
  • Search engine list and default engine
  • Wallpaper settings (including base64 data of locally uploaded wallpapers, compressed)
  • Weather settings (city name and coordinates, temperature unit)
  • Widget layout and widget data (to-do items, notes text, pomodoro timer state, translation languages, chosen trending source, system monitor settings)
  • Appearance and general preferences (interface language, clock, greeting nickname, etc.)

About location: the weather widget uses "auto location" by default. The extension declares the geolocation permission and asks the browser for your coordinates when the weather widget or the clock weather badge is displayed; the browser (and your operating system) stays in control of the permission and you can deny it at any time. The coordinates are used solely to query weather and the city name from the services above and are never stored on any server — they are cached in your own browser for at most 30 minutes so that opening a new tab does not re-locate you every time. If location is unavailable, the extension falls back to an approximate city inferred from your IP address, and then to a city you can pick yourself; switch the location mode to "Choose city" and the extension stops requesting your location entirely.

Exporting and Deleting Your Data

  • In Settings → Data you can export all settings as a JSON file, export your browser bookmarks as an HTML file, or reset to defaults. Exported files are yours to keep.
  • Uninstalling the extension automatically removes all data stored locally by the browser.

Children's Privacy

This extension is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please reach out via the support contact listed on the extension's store page.

SimNewTab 隐私政策

最后更新日期：2026 年 9 月 26 日

SimNewTab（以下简称「本扩展」）是一款浏览器新标签页扩展。我们非常重视你的隐私。本政策说明本扩展如何处理数据。

总览

本扩展没有账号系统、没有自己的服务器、没有统计埋点、没有广告、没有追踪代码。

你的全部个性化配置仅保存在你自己的浏览器本地存储（chrome.storage.local）中，不会上传到我们的服务器。扩展运行中会按需向下列第三方公开服务发起请求，这些请求仅用于提供相应功能，本扩展不向任何一方发送你的身份信息。

数据的本地存储

以下数据仅存于浏览器本地，不会离开你的设备（除非你主动使用导出功能）：

  • 快捷方式列表（名称、网址、图标与配色）
  • 搜索引擎列表与默认引擎
  • 壁纸设置（含本地上传壁纸压缩后的 base64 数据）
  • 天气设置（城市名与坐标、温度单位）
  • 小组件布局与组件数据（待办事项、笔记正文、番茄钟计时状态、翻译语言、所选热搜数据源、监控条设置）
  • 外观与通用偏好（界面语言、时钟、问候语昵称等）

activeTab仅在你点击上面那个右键菜单项的瞬间，用于读取当前标签页的网址与标题以预填快捷方式表单。activeTab 只对这一次用户主动操作临时生效，因此扩展不会常驻「读取浏览记录」的权限
